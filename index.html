About GMSA UG

The Ghana Muslim Students Association, University of Ghana (GMSA UG) is a faith-based student organisation dedicated to the spiritual, academic, and social development of Muslim students at the University of Ghana, Legon. We unite students through Islamic values, community service, and shared purpose.
